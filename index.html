MarketWhy Beginner's Financial Metric Guide

Market Capitalisation (Market Cap)

Total dollar value of all a company's shares combined (Share Price × Total Shares). Measures company size rather than whether a single share is "cheap" or "expensive."

P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings)

How much investors pay for every $1 of annual company profit. A high P/E (e.g., 45x) means the market expects rapid future growth; a low P/E (e.g., 12x) signals mature or slower growth.

52-Week High & Low

The highest and lowest prices the stock has traded at over the past 12 months. Helps show whether a stock is near peak momentum or recovering from a pullback.

Revenue Growth vs. Net Margin

Revenue Growth measures how fast top-line sales are expanding. Net Profit Margin shows what percentage of sales remains as actual profit after all expenses and taxes.

Confirmed Facts vs. Explanations

Stock prices move on expectations. MarketWhy separates verified filings (earnings beats, guidance) from plausible market drivers (sector rotation, interest rates).

US vs. ASX (.AX) Tickers

US stocks trade in USD on NASDAQ/NYSE (e.g., NVDA). Australian stocks trade in AUD on the ASX and carry the .AX suffix (e.g., BHP.AX).
